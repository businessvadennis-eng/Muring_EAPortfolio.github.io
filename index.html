+
I'm based in Davao City, Philippines, and routinely work US (EST, CST, PST), AU, and PH hours. I'll align with whatever rhythm your team needs.

+
Both available. Most clients start at 20 hours a week and scale up. Full-time dedicated support is offered to a maximum of 2 clients at a time.
How do you handle confidentiality?
+
NDA-first. Encrypted password sharing via 1Password or Bitwarden. Access is least-privilege and revocable on day one if needed.

+
Flat monthly retainers based on scope. We'll size the right package on the discovery call. No surprise invoices and no hour-counting.
Can I see references?
+
Yes. I share named client references after the discovery call so we can both confirm fit first.
