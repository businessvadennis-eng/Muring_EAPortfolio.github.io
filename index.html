Case study · Crisis management

A storm, a roof leak and a dead A/C, fixed remotely in 24 hours.

During a summer heatwave, a storm caused a roof leak at one of the CEO's rental homes. Water shorted out the A/C, the tenant had no cooling, and I was managing it all from a different time zone.

01

Remote triage

Walked the tenant through water containment and safety steps while I reviewed the layout and vendor options.

02

Two trades dispatched

A roofer for emergency tarping and repair, an HVAC tech for the electrical fault. Lockbox access for both.

03

Sequenced the fix

Roofer first to stop the water, then HVAC to repair the blower motor and prevent more damage.

04

Closed the loop

Approved invoices, reviewed post-repair photos, and confirmed with the tenant that everything worked.

24 hrsBoth issues resolved within a day, with no interior drywall damage and zero downtime complaints from the tenant.
