Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: this is a personal portfolio site. There are no accounts, no newsletter, no ads and no tracking or analytics. The only time I get your details is when you choose to book a call or email me, and I use them only to reply to you.

Who runs this site

This site is the portfolio of Cio (Dennis Canales Muring), a freelance executive assistant based in the Philippines. If you have a question about your information, email clientcontact.cio@gmail.com.

What I don’t collect

  • No sign-ups, accounts or mailing lists.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies.
  • I don’t sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

What you might send me

Booking a call. If you use the booking form, you give me your name, email, the time you picked and an optional note about what you need help with. The form sends this to my inbox through a form service called FormSubmit. I use it only to confirm the call and reply to you.

Emailing me. If you email me directly, I get whatever you include in your message, and I use it only to reply.

I keep these messages in my email for as long as I need them to follow up or work with you. If you’d like me to delete them, just ask.

Things stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers your choice (using local storage) so the site looks the same next time. It stays on your device and isn’t sent to me.

Outside services this site loads

Like most websites, this one loads a few things from other services. When your browser loads them, those services may see basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type. They have their own privacy policies.

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr: provide an animation library and some logo images.
  • FormSubmit: delivers booking requests to my inbox, only if you send one.

Links to LinkedIn and OnlineJobs.ph take you to their sites, which have their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask me what I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email clientcontact.cio@gmail.com and I’ll take care of it.

Children

This site is meant for business owners and professionals. It isn’t aimed at children, and I don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
